FAQ

Questions, answered

Is GramLingo free?
Yes — play free right now. No paywall between you and the first grammar world.
Do I need to take a test first?
No. There are no placement tests — the game itself learns from your mistakes.
Can I study in Chinese?
Yes — switch between English, 中文 and Español anytime, even mid-lesson.
Who is GramLingo for?
Anyone aiming for IELTS, CET-4 or gaokao — or anyone who just wants English that actually sticks. Pick your goal, play, and the quest meets you there.
Does it work on my phone?
Yes — it runs in any browser on phone, tablet or desktop. No download, no install, and your progress follows you.
How much time do I need?
Short sessions work — even five minutes. The quest saves your place and your wrong-book builds your review automatically.
